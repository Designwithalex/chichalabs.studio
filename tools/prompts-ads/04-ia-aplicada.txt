Dark editorial tech visual, rendered at high-fidelity 3D product-visualization
quality. Square 1:1 composition, 1080x1080 minimum, sharp to the edges.

SUBJECT — the boring work, done without a person doing it.

LEFT OF FRAME: a tall, leaning, unstable stack of pale grey documents. Dozens of
sheets, edges misaligned, slightly translucent, the stack receding upward and out
of the top of the frame to imply it never ends. It should carry weight and dread.

A horizontal mint-green scanning plane cuts through the stack at mid-height — a
thin blade of light with a soft bloom above and below it. Sheets passing through
that plane visibly change state: grey and crumpled below the line, clean and
precisely aligned above it. The state change is the mechanism of the image.

RIGHT OF FRAME: the processed sheets arrive as three neat floating glass cards at
staggered depths. Each card carries a small mint monospace label chip and a tidy
row of extracted key-value lines rendered as abstract text bars. Perfectly
ordered, calm, finished.

The intelligence is invisible — only its result is visible. Absolutely no robot,
no android hand, no glowing brain, no humanoid figure of any kind.

PALETTE — strict. Near-black canvas #0A0A0B. Layered surfaces #111214 and
#16181C. One single accent: mint green #2CE58B, reserved for the scanning plane
and the label chips. Secondary green #1FA968 for depth only. The processed sheets
and UI chrome #F5F6F7. The raw document stack and all muted labels #A1A1AA.

TREATMENT — the result cards use glassmorphism: 4% white fill, 1px borders at 10%
white, heavy background blur, 16px corner radius. Soft mint bloom behind the
scanning plane, roughly #2CE58B at 35% opacity with 30px spread — a glow, never a
hard neon outline. Volumetric haze in the deep background. Very subtle film
grain. A faint 1px grid in white at 4% opacity receding into the dark.

LIGHTING — one cool key light from upper left, mint rim light from lower right,
deep unlit shadows. The scanning plane is its own light source. Cinematic,
low-key, high contrast.

COMPOSITION — heavy mass on the left, calm order on the right, the scanning plane
as the horizontal axis that divides them. Generous negative space. Asymmetric and
editorial.

TYPOGRAPHY — no readable words anywhere. All document content and extracted
fields are abstract horizontal bars. The label chips carry short monospace
glyph-marks, not legible words. This is deliberate.

MOOD — this used to take a person all morning.

AVOID — stock-photo people, handshakes, office scenes, blue corporate palettes,
purple-cyan AI gradients, glowing brains, neural-network node clouds, circuit
boards, robots, androids, lens flares, bevelled 3D, rainbow gradients,
watermarks, real company logos, any second accent hue.
