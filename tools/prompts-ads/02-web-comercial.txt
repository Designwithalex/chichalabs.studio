Dark editorial tech visual, rendered at high-fidelity 3D product-visualization
quality. Square 1:1 composition, 1080x1080 minimum, sharp to the edges.

SUBJECT — the difference between being visited and being contacted.

Two floating dark glass browser panels in 3D space, staggered in depth, seen at
a slight three-quarter angle.

The BACK panel is desaturated and dim: a generic website wireframe in flat grey,
lifeless. Around it, dozens of small grey particles drift past and away,
dispersing off-frame. They arrive and they leave. Nothing lands.

The FRONT panel is alive: the same wireframe but rendered crisply in near-white
on a dark surface, wrapped in a soft mint-green bloom. The grey particles that
reach this panel turn mint on contact and funnel into a converging stream that
narrows into a single glowing point at the panel's edge — where a small glass
notification card has just appeared, tilted forward, catching the light.

A thin mint line rises across the front panel like a metric climbing.

The contrast between the two panels is the entire subject: dead traffic on one
side, captured intent on the other. No people, no hands, no cursors.

PALETTE — strict. Near-black canvas #0A0A0B. Layered surfaces #111214 and
#16181C. One single accent: mint green #2CE58B, reserved for the converging
particles, the rising line and the notification card. Secondary green #1FA968
for depth only. UI chrome #F5F6F7. Muted greys and the dead particles #A1A1AA.

TREATMENT — floating panels with glassmorphism: 4% white fill, 1px borders at
10% white, heavy background blur, 16px corner radius. Soft mint bloom behind
accent elements, roughly #2CE58B at 35% opacity with 30px spread — a glow, never
a hard neon outline. Volumetric haze in the deep background. Very subtle film
grain. A faint 1px grid in white at 4% opacity receding into the dark.

LIGHTING — one cool key light from upper left, mint rim light from lower right,
deep unlit shadows. Cinematic, low-key, high contrast.

COMPOSITION — generous negative space, strong depth layering between the two
panels, the funnel point as the single focal centre of gravity. Asymmetric and
editorial. Nothing centered.

TYPOGRAPHY — no readable text anywhere. Represent all interface copy as abstract
horizontal bars of varying length. This is deliberate: no lettering at all.

MOOD — this one actually converts.

AVOID — stock-photo people, handshakes, office scenes, blue corporate palettes,
purple-cyan AI gradients, glowing brains, circuit boards, robots, cluttered
dashboards, lens flares, bevelled 3D, rainbow gradients, watermarks, real
company logos, any second accent hue.
