Dark editorial tech visual, rendered at high-fidelity 3D product-visualization
quality. Square 1:1 composition, 1080x1080 minimum, sharp to the edges.

SUBJECT — a spreadsheet becoming an instrument. One continuous left-to-right
transformation across the frame.

LEFT THIRD: a dense, oppressive spreadsheet grid. Hundreds of tiny grey cells,
slightly warped and sagging under their own weight, some cells cracked, the outer
edges fraying into loose fragments. Colourless, claustrophobic, illegible. It
should read as a burden.

MIDDLE: the fragments lift off the grid and reorganise in mid-air, caught in
motion — cell shards rotating, aligning, catching mint-green rim light as they
turn. This is the hinge of the image.

RIGHT TWO-THIRDS: the shards land as a calm, spacious dark glass dashboard panel.
Only three or four large clean modules: one crisp mint bar series, one thin
ascending line, one oversized monospace number with a small mint delta triangle
beside it. Enormous breathing room between the modules. Deliberately
under-populated — the visual opposite of the chaos on the left.

The eye should travel left to right and feel physical relief. No people, no
hands, no cursors.

PALETTE — strict. Near-black canvas #0A0A0B. Layered surfaces #111214 and
#16181C. One single accent: mint green #2CE58B, appearing only on the rim light
of the flying shards, the bar series, the ascending line and the delta.
Secondary green #1FA968 for depth only. The big number and UI chrome #F5F6F7.
The dead spreadsheet and all muted labels #A1A1AA.

TREATMENT — the dashboard panel uses glassmorphism: 4% white fill, 1px borders at
10% white, heavy background blur, 16px corner radius. Soft mint bloom behind
accent elements, roughly #2CE58B at 35% opacity with 30px spread — a glow, never
a hard neon outline. Volumetric haze in the deep background. Very subtle film
grain. A faint 1px grid in white at 4% opacity receding into the dark.

LIGHTING — one cool key light from upper left, mint rim light from lower right,
deep unlit shadows. Cinematic, low-key, high contrast.

COMPOSITION — the transformation runs horizontally across the full width.
Generous negative space on the right. Asymmetric and editorial.

TYPOGRAPHY — the only readable element is the large number, rendered in a clean
geometric monospace as plain placeholder digits with no currency symbol and no
words. Every other label is an abstract bar. Crisp and correctly kerned.

MOOD — from nobody understands it to anybody can read it.

AVOID — stock-photo people, handshakes, office scenes, blue corporate palettes,
purple-cyan AI gradients, glowing brains, circuit boards, robots, cluttered
dashboards with dozens of widgets, lens flares, bevelled 3D, rainbow gradients,
watermarks, real company logos, any second accent hue.
