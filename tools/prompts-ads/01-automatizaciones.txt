Dark editorial tech visual, rendered at high-fidelity 3D product-visualization
quality. Square 1:1 composition, 1080x1080 minimum, sharp to the edges.

SUBJECT — the moment an inquiry gets answered while nobody is watching.

Foreground left: a floating dark glass phone-shaped panel, tilted a few degrees
in 3D space, showing one incoming chat bubble in muted grey with a monospace
timestamp "21:47" beside it. The bubble is small and unremarkable — it is the
thing that would normally be missed.

From that bubble a single mint-green line of light exits to the right and
immediately branches into three clean paths that connect to three small glass
node cards floating at different depths. Each node carries one minimal glyph
only: a checkmark, a database cylinder, a bell. A bright pulse of light is
visibly travelling along the middle path, caught mid-motion with a soft trail
behind it.

The outermost node emits a small mint reply bubble heading back toward the
phone, closing the loop.

The whole flow reads left to right as one uninterrupted circuit: the message
came in and the system already handled it. No human, no hand, no cursor
anywhere in frame. Deep hazed void behind everything.

PALETTE — strict. Near-black canvas #0A0A0B. Layered surfaces #111214 and
#16181C. One single accent: mint green #2CE58B, used only on the flow of light
and the active glyphs, never as decoration. Secondary green #1FA968 for depth
only. Bright elements and UI chrome #F5F6F7. Muted labels #A1A1AA.

TREATMENT — floating panels with glassmorphism: 4% white fill, 1px borders at
10% white, heavy background blur, 16px corner radius. Soft mint bloom behind
accent elements, roughly #2CE58B at 35% opacity with 30px spread — a glow, never
a hard neon outline. Volumetric haze in the deep background. Very subtle film
grain. A faint 1px grid in white at 4% opacity receding into the dark.

LIGHTING — one cool key light from upper left, mint rim light from lower right,
deep unlit shadows. Cinematic, low-key, high contrast.

COMPOSITION — generous negative space, one clear focal point, strong depth
layering: foreground panel, midground nodes, hazed background. Asymmetric and
editorial. Nothing centered.

TYPOGRAPHY — the only text is the timestamp "21:47" in a clean geometric
monospace. Crisp and correctly kerned. No other lettering.

MOOD — quiet competence. It works while you sleep.
